About Present of Work

Present of Work is a learning commons—a third space where practitioners exploring work as participation in life on earth gather to practice gift economy, peer learning, and collective stewardship. Like fish discovering water, we can't see our work culture until we step into a different stream.

The name holds three meanings: work as present (gift), being present (attentive), making alternatives present (manifest by embodying them).

This isn't random organizing—it's creating a counter-environment to extractive professional culture. The medium is the message. The commons doesn't teach these principles—it IS them. Structure shapes practitioners. Practitioners shape organizations. This is how transformation spreads.
