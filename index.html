Can you accompany transformation
without extracting from it?

Limicelia is part of the Present of Work movement—a network of practitioners who assemble to walk with organizations, communities, and movements through threshold moments.

Limicelia — From Latin limen (threshold) and celia (hidden chamber). The practice of attending to what emerges at boundaries, staying present through transformation without forcing the outcome.

Our Approach

We don't believe in silver bullets. Transformation happens through sustained accompaniment, not quick fixes. We work with what's present, not what "should" be there.

Start With Relationship

How do we begin transformation work?

+

Before strategy, before structure, before new practices—we begin by understanding the relational field. Who trusts whom? Where's the resilience? Where's the friction? Transformation happens through relationship, not around it.

Attend to Emergence

What about planning and strategy?

+

We don't impose predetermined solutions. Instead, we create conditions for what needs to emerge. This requires patience, skill at pattern recognition, and trust that groups hold their own answers—they just need the right containers to discover them.

Build Capacity, Not Dependency

What happens when the work ends?

+

When we leave, you should be more capable than when we arrived. We transfer practices, not just results. We work alongside your team, not above them. The work itself becomes the training ground for new ways of being together.

Integrate Multiple Ways of Knowing

Is intellectual understanding enough?

+

Cognitive understanding is necessary but not sufficient. We engage embodied wisdom, emotional intelligence, relational knowing, and systems awareness. Different situations require different ways of knowing—and groups that can access multiple modes are more resilient.

Work at Multiple Scales

Where does change happen?

+

Personal transformation, team dynamics, organizational culture, network coordination—these aren't separate challenges. They're nested systems. We work across scales simultaneously, recognizing that change at one level affects all others.

Center Justice & Liberation

What about power and equity?

+

Transformation work that doesn't address power, privilege, and systemic oppression isn't transformation—it's rearranging deck chairs. We bring explicit analysis of how dominant culture patterns show up in organizations and work to dismantle them.

How We Work With You

We walk alongside those navigating change—organizations, communities, movements, teams. Staying present as things unfold, adapting as patterns reveal themselves.

What Accompaniment Produces

Real outcomes from working with mission-driven organizations on transformation.

Funder Network Collaboration

The Challenge: A network of funders working on peace and global security struggled to coordinate across different priorities and geographies. They wanted to collaborate but lacked shared frameworks for decision-making and communication.

What We Did: Over 6 months, we facilitated shared decision-making practices, created communication protocols, designed how funders could learn from each other without centralizing decision-making, and built capacity for distributed coordination.

Outcomes: The network renewed their partnership commitment. Decision quality improved. Participants reported feeling less isolated, more connected to shared mission. They chose to continue the partnership and expand their collaboration.

What "Accompaniment" Means

We don't parachute in with recommendations. We work alongside your team—listening more than telling, building your capacity rather than creating dependency, treating the work itself as the practice where transformation happens.

After we leave, you have not just new practices, but the relational foundation and distributed understanding to evolve them. The transformation stays because your people own it.

How We Handle Resources

We need resources to sustain this work. And we're committed to accessibility for those building capacity. How do we honor both?

Fee-for-Service Work

Organizations with resource capacity pay for accompaniment at rates that reflect the deep, sustained nature of the work. These fees support practitioner compensation, infrastructure, subsidizing accessibility, and building commons resources.

Sliding Scale

Grassroots organizations and community groups access services on a sliding scale based on actual capacity. We ask what's genuinely sustainable for you.

Gift-Based Offerings

Monthly practice spaces and some workshops operate on gift economy principles. Come if called. Give what you can. This isn't charity; it's reciprocity operating on different timelines.

What We Ask

If you have resources: Pay what allows practitioners to sustain this work and creates capacity for others.

If you're building capacity: Be honest about what's sustainable. We'll find a way that honors both.

For everyone: Recognize that economic relationships are relational. How we handle money together matters.

About Present of Work

Present of Work is a learning network reimagining work as sacred practice rather than industrial extraction. The name holds three meanings: being fully present, working in the present moment rather than anxious future projection, and work as gift—an offering that strengthens the web of life.

The Ecosystem

🌱 Soil/Commons

Free resources accessible to all: monthly practice spaces (CommDojo, Conflict Kinship Dojo, Soul Space), open-source toolkits, peer learning podcasts, community calls. Gift economy accessible.

🪢 Knots (Learning Guilds)

Intensive 8-12 week programs: Organizational Emergence Guild, Economic Livelihood Guild, Warm Data Guild, Communications Guild. Deep skill development in cohorts.

🕸️ Social Web (Labs)

ATEM (Alternatives to Extractive Methods) Labs exploring systems transformation. Current: Reimagining Philanthropy—bringing together funders, recipients, and intermediaries.

How Limicelia and Present of Work Work Together

Limicelia is a nonprofit that does fee-for-service organizational transformation work. Present of Work is a learning network that operates gift-based communities and pursues grants for capacity building programs.

Resources from Limicelia's consulting work flow to support Present of Work's gift-based offerings and accessibility. This structure allows both to thrive: Limicelia can ask organizations with capacity to support the work sustainably, while Present of Work can offer learning on gift and sliding scale principles. It's one integrated practice, aligned economics.
