We've seen the rise of: • Reimagining the world (SDGs, systems change) • Reinventing organizations (Teal, holacracy, distributed models) • Inner development (consciousness, mindfulness) But we've fragmented the question. You can't reimagine the world without reimagining work. You can't reimagine work without reimagining what it means to be human in relationship. Present of Work is a learning commons addressing this integration. We explore work as participation in life on earth through gift-stewarded space, peer learning, and collective governance—not as concepts, but as lived practice. Why commons? Because "the medium is the message" (McLuhan). The structure itself teaches. When practitioners participate in gift economy, they don't learn about it—they embody it. That's what they bring to organizational work. This is movement building through form, not ideology. Learn more: https://limicelia.org/present-of-work #TransformationWork #OrganizationalDevelopment #Commons #FutureOfWork #SystemsChange

💼 LinkedIn

Most professional development contradicts itself: hierarchical teaching about distributed authority, transactional courses about gift economy, extractive consulting about regenerative practices. The form contradicts the message. And when form and content conflict, form always wins. I just read "The Medium is the Teaching" - an essay applying Marshall McLuhan's framework to organizational transformation work. The core insight: Present of Work is organized as commons not to teach these principles but to BE them. The structure is the curriculum. When practitioners participate in gift-stewarded space, peer learning, and collective governance as lived practice (not concepts), they embody these patterns. That's what they bring to organizational work. This is movement building through form, not ideology. Creating counter-environments that make dominant culture visible through contrast. Strategic organizing at scale. Worth reading if you work in organizational development, systems change, or transformation practice: https://limicelia.org/blog/the-medium-is-the-teaching #OrganizationalDevelopment #SystemsChange #TransformationWork #McLuhan #Commons
