## KEY FRAMEWORKS

### Financial Reality

**Cost of Living Requirements**:
- Carol (CX): $150-200K annually (single-income household)
- BK: $125-175K annually (single-income household)
- **Combined requirement**: $275-375K minimum
- **Target revenue**: $350-450K total

### Entity Structure

**Limicelia**: 
- Consulting practice generating ALL revenue (fees, grants, donations)
- 501c3 or fiscally sponsored nonprofit
- Carol + BK as co-stewards

**Present of Work**: 
- Knowledge commons, volunteer-run
- Carol + BK participate but don't own
- Separate organizing team (to be formed)
- **Relationship**: Reciprocal (revenue supports practitioners, commons shapes how they work)

### Capacity Model

**Per Steward Annual Allocation**:
- 1,500 hours: Limicelia revenue work (can reallocate quarterly)
- 250 hours: Present of Work participation
- 90 hours: Admin/operations

**Flexible bucket model** allows reallocation based on actual client work.

## SERVICE OFFERINGS

### Limicelia Services

**1. Sustained Accompaniment** (3-6 months, $50-75K)
- Bi-weekly/monthly sessions
- Capacity transfer, not solution delivery
- Organizations continue independently after engagement

**2. Facilitated Gatherings** (1-3 days, $10-18K)
- Strategic retreats, stakeholder dialogues
- Conflict navigation, network convenings

**3. Systems Convening** ($60-100K total, coordinated with partners)
- Multi-practitioner teams
- Limicelia takes 25-30% coordination fee
- Specialized practitioners deliver expertise

**4. Grant-Funded Programs** ($40-80K per program)
- Foundation-funded cohort work
- 3-8 organizations receive capacity-building

## DISCOVERY QUESTIONS

### For Organizations

**What to Ask in Customer Discovery Conversations**:
1. What challenges are you experiencing right now?
2. How are you currently addressing these challenges?
3. What would success look like?
4. What's at stake if challenges continue?
5. How do you currently budget for this type of support?
6. What's your timeline for addressing this?
7. Who else needs to be involved in the decision?
8. Have you worked with consultants before? What worked/didn't?

## COMMUNICATION PREFERENCES

### Carol's Pattern
Prefers being understood over misunderstood, may offer additional context. Recognizes it's not her place to control others' understanding. Appreciates signals that something has been understood sufficiently.

### BK's Pattern
Prefers not repeating information already shared. Recognizes pattern of making compromises that violate boundaries. Actively developing capacity to name when process feels harmful and request course correction earlier.

### How We Work With These
- Name patterns with curiosity, not judgment
- Carol checks: "Have I explained sufficiently?"
- BK practices: "This crosses a boundary, need to pause"
- Recognize patterns as information about needs, not flaws
- Support each other's growth edges without requiring change
