# LIMICELIA CO-STEWARD CONSTITUTION

*A living document articulating our shared framings and individual autonomy*

---

## PREAMBLE

Limicelia operates through co-stewardship, recognizing that **Carol (CX)** and **BK** bring different gifts, interests, and ways of being to this work. This constitution acknowledges that **alignment on everything is neither necessary nor desirable**. Instead, we articulate shared framings providing container for individual expression, collaborative experimentation, and honest difference.

We recognize that **work is participation in life, not extraction from it**. Our co-stewardship embodies this through practicing together what we offer organizations: distributed authority, emergent strategy, and threshold-crossing as ongoing practice.

---

## SHARED FRAMINGS

### What We Stand For

**Form is Politics**  
How we organize matters more than what we say. We practice distributed authority, refuse extraction, and organize as commons where possible. The medium is the message.

**Threshold-Crossing Not Problem-Solving**  
Organizations don't need consultants fixing problems. They need accompaniment through thresholds where they become different. We walk alongside, building capacity for ongoing navigation.

**Accompaniment Not Consulting**  
We don't deliver solutions and leave. We transfer capacity, practice together, and ensure organizations can continue without us. No dependency creation.

**Liberation Through Practice**  
"You can't liberate through forms that reproduce domination." (Bayo Akomolafe) We practice gift economy, peer learning, and collective governance so we bring embodied experience to organizational work, not just techniques.

**Meeting Not Matching**  
We approach each year, each organization, each practitioner where they are - meeting them in their reality, not matching external benchmarks or forcing predetermined models.

---

### What We Refuse

**Extraction as Business Model**  
No recurring revenue models where we benefit from client dependency. No scaling at all costs. No growth requiring burnout.

**Efficiency Over Emergence**  
The times are urgent; let us slow down. We refuse to compress threshold-crossing into deliverables and timelines that prevent genuine becoming.

**Separation of Head, Heart, Body**  
Work engaging only intellect is incomplete. We bring contemplative practice, somatic awareness, and relational presence. Whole humans accompanying whole humans.

**Invisible Power Dynamics**  
We name and work with power - in organizations, in movements, in our own collaboration. Justice through form, not ideology.

---

### Our Commitments

**Financial Sustainability**  
We sustain ourselves at livable wages appropriate to our cost of living contexts. Carol and BK are primary earners in single-income households. This isn't greed - it's recognizing that practitioners need material foundation to do deep work.

**Values Over Revenue**  
We say no to extractive work even when we need money. We maintain sliding scale access for grassroots organizations even when premium pricing would be easier. Our integrity matters more than our income.

**Long-Term Orientation**  
We build for 5-10 year timeframe, not quarterly metrics. Patient capital, slow cooking, emergence over execution.

**Mutual Support Not Competition**  
We strengthen each other's practice. We don't compete for clients or hide knowledge. We share learning, refer opportunities, celebrate each other's growth.

## INDIVIDUAL AUTONOMY

### Recognizing Difference

**Carol** and **BK** are drawn to different territories, methods, and ways of being. This is strength, not problem. We don't require sameness.

**What This Means**:
- We may accept different client types or focus on different services
- We may facilitate using different methods and drawing from different lineages  
- We may have different opinions on political, strategic, or tactical questions
- We may participate differently in Present of Work or other commons spaces
- We may write/speak from different perspectives or emphasize different themes
- We have different levels of comfort with public visibility and privacy

**What We Require**:
- Transparency about our differences (internal honesty)
- Respect for each other's autonomy (not undermining)
- Clarity with clients about who's working with them and how
- Commitment to shared financial agreements
- Honor each other's boundaries around privacy and public presence

---

### Communication Preferences & Working Patterns

We acknowledge that Carol and BK have different communication styles and working patterns. Understanding these helps us collaborate more effectively.

**Carol's Patterns**:
- **Prefers understanding over being misunderstood**, while recognizing it's not her place to control how others understand or interpret her work
- Values clarity and directness in communication
- Appreciates opportunities to provide context when needed
- Recognizes that people will form their own interpretations and meanings

**BK's Patterns**:
- **Dislikes repeating themselves**, which can sometimes lead to agreeing to things that violate boundaries or goals
- Working on recognizing when a process feels harmful and stopping it earlier rather than letting it escalate
- Values efficiency in communication and decision-making
- Appreciates when decisions stick and don't require revisiting

**How We Work With These Patterns**:
- We check in regularly about whether we're aligned on decisions
- We give each other permission to say "this doesn't feel right" before resentment builds
- We recognize that Carol may need to articulate context while BK may want to move forward
- We balance Carol's desire for clarity with BK's preference for not rehashing
- When we disagree, we ask: "Is this difference healthy or is it creating tension that prevents work?"

**What This Means in Practice**:
- Carol can request opportunities to provide context without expecting BK to change understanding
- BK can flag when a conversation is becoming repetitive and suggest moving forward
- Both recognize when they're compromising boundaries and speak up before harm occurs
- We treat these patterns with kindness - they're not failures, they're how we each navigate the world

## DECISION-MAKING PROTOCOLS

### Consent-Based Authority

**Major Decisions** (require both stewards' consent):
- Taking on client work using "Limicelia" name
- Setting pricing or payment terms for organizational work
- Committing organizational resources (money, time, partnerships)
- Public statements on behalf of Limicelia
- Hiring or contracting other practitioners under Limicelia
- Changes to business structure or legal status
- Marketing/website content representing both stewards

**Individual Authority** (can be made solo):
- Content creation using own name/voice
- Speaking engagements as individual (not "representing Limicelia")
- Individual coaching or consulting not using Limicelia brand
- Present of Work participation and organizing
- Personal practice experimentation
- Professional development and learning
- Social media presence and public engagement (respecting privacy preferences)

**Consent Process**:
1. Steward proposes decision in writing (can be informal)
2. Other steward has 72 hours to respond
3. If no objection = decision proceeds
4. If objection = dialogue until resolution or proposal withdrawn
5. If cannot resolve = escalate to mediation

## EVOLUTION & ADAPTATION

### This Constitution is Living

We revisit and revise annually or when either steward requests review. Changes require consent of both stewards.

**Questions We Ask**:
- Are our shared framings still true?
- Are we honoring individual autonomy?
- Are decision-making protocols working?
- Are we respecting each other's boundaries (privacy, capacity, focus)?
- Are we working with our communication patterns kindly?
- What needs to change?

### When Difference Becomes Conflict

We distinguish:
- **Difference** = We see/approach things differently (healthy, to be expected)
- **Conflict** = Our differences create tension preventing work (needs attention)

When conflict arises, we engage our conflict resolution protocols with recognition that:
- Carol's desire for understanding and context is valid
- BK's desire to move forward without repetition is valid
- Both can honor boundaries before resentment builds
- We're learning together how to navigate differences
