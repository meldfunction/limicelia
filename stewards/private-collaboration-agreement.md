## 1. FINANCIAL ARRANGEMENTS

### 1.1 Revenue Sharing

**Default Split: 50/50**  
All Limicelia revenue is split evenly unless otherwise agreed for specific projects.

**What This Covers**:
- Sustained accompaniment engagements either steward leads
- Facilitated gatherings either steward delivers
- Grant funding received by Limicelia
- Donations to Limicelia
- Any other organizational income

**When Split Changes**:
If one steward does significantly more work on specific engagement, stewards can agree to different split (e.g., 60/40, 70/30). This must be discussed and agreed **before accepting the work**.

**Tracking**: Both stewards have access to shared financial spreadsheet showing all income, expenses, and distribution.

---

### 1.2 Expense Handling

**Shared Expenses** (deducted before distribution):
- Business insurance
- Website hosting and tools
- Professional development relevant to Limicelia work
- Marketing/content creation costs
- Travel for joint client work
- Accounting/bookkeeping services
- Present of Work seed support (if budget allows)

**Individual Expenses** (each steward covers):
- Personal health insurance
- Individual coaching/therapy
- Personal professional development
- Travel for individual work
- Individual equipment (laptops, etc.)

**Approval Process**:
- Expenses under $200: Either steward can approve
- Expenses $200-1000: Requires discussion and agreement
- Expenses over $1000: Requires formal consent and documented decision

---

### 1.3 Compensation Commitments

**Minimum Viable Income**:
- Carol: $50K annually (mortgage, childcare, basic living)
- BK: $40K annually (rent, basic living)
- **Total: $90K minimum before considering other expenses**

**Priority Cascade**:
1. Meet minimum viable income for both stewards
2. Cover essential business expenses
3. Build 3-month financial runway
4. Support Present of Work seed funding
5. Build savings for lean periods
6. Increase compensation above minimum

**If Revenue Falls Short**:
- First: Reduce/pause Present of Work support
- Second: Reduce optional business expenses
- Third: Take on additional client work (even if less aligned)
- Fourth: Consider if Limicelia is viable

**Quarterly Financial Review**:
- Assess whether meeting minimum income
- Project next quarter's revenue
- Adjust effort/priorities as needed

---

### 1.4 Individual Client Work

**Using Limicelia Brand**:
- All work presented as "Limicelia" shares revenue 50/50
- Client proposals/contracts under Limicelia name require both stewards' consent
- Marketing/outreach using Limicelia brand represents both stewards

**Using Individual Name**:
- Either steward can do individual consulting under own name
- Revenue from individual work belongs to that steward (not split)
- Must not compete with or undermine Limicelia positioning
- Should not confuse clients about what's Limicelia vs. individual work

**Gray Areas**:
- If client came through Limicelia network/reputation: Discuss revenue sharing
- If work overlaps with Limicelia services: Bring under Limicelia umbrella
- When unsure: Default to transparency and dialogue

### 2.2 Client Work Distribution

**Lead vs. Support Roles**:
- **Lead**: Primary relationship holder, main facilitator, project manager
- **Support**: Contributes to design, co-facilitates sessions, provides input

**Default Approach**:
- Sustained accompaniment: One steward leads, other supports or solo
- Facilitated gatherings: Solo or co-facilitation depending on complexity
- Grant-funded programs: Negotiate role distribution based on fit and capacity

**Saying No**:
Either steward can decline to participate in specific client work:
- Too far from personal interests/gifts
- Insufficient capacity
- Values misalignment with client
- Personal conflict with organization/people

**When One Declines**:
- Other steward can proceed solo (keeps 100% revenue from that work)
- Or decline the opportunity entirely
- No obligation to take on work partner doesn't want

---

### 2.3 Vacation & Personal Time

**Time Off Commitments**:
- Each steward takes minimum 3 weeks vacation annually
- Personal/family emergencies take priority over client work
- Burnout prevention over revenue maximization

**Coverage Protocol**:
- Advance notice when possible (4+ weeks for planned time off)
- For sustained work: Either pause or other steward covers critical sessions
- For facilitated gatherings: Reschedule or other steward covers if possible
- Clients are informed about two-steward model upfront

## 3. CONFLICT RESOLUTION

### 3.1 Types of Conflict

We distinguish between:

**Type A: Difference Without Tension**  
Different opinions, approaches, or interests that don't prevent collaboration.  
**Response**: Acknowledge difference, honor autonomy, continue working.

**Type B: Productive Conflict**  
Disagreement that surfaces important issues and leads to better decisions.  
**Response**: Engage fully, explore perspectives, work toward resolution.

**Type C: Destructive Conflict**  
Tension preventing effective work, damaging relationship, or creating suffering.  
**Response**: Use escalating conflict resolution protocols below.

---

### 3.2 Conflict Resolution Stages

**Stage 1: Direct Dialogue (First Attempt)**

When conflict arises:
1. Either steward can request "conflict conversation" (in writing or verbally)
2. Schedule within 72 hours
3. Use format:
   - Each person shares their experience without interruption (10 min each)
   - Each person reflects back what they heard (5 min each)  
   - Explore what's needed for resolution (open-ended dialogue)
   - Name any agreements or next steps

**Stage 2: Structured Process (If Stage 1 Doesn't Resolve)**

If direct dialogue doesn't resolve conflict within 2 weeks:
1. Use written reflection process:
   - Each steward writes: What happened, my experience, what I need, what I'm willing to offer
   - Exchange writings, sit with them for 48 hours
   - Meet to discuss what emerged
   
2. If still unresolved, try specific method:
   - Nonviolent Communication format
   - Restorative circle with trusted third party as witness
   - Somatic/body-based dialogue
   - Choose method together

**Stage 3: Mediation (If Stage 2 Doesn't Resolve)**

If conflict persists after structured process:
1. Identify neutral third-party mediator (someone both trust)
2. Mediator facilitates dialogue session (2-4 hours)
3. Work toward either:
   - Resolution allowing continued collaboration
   - Clear understanding that collaboration should pause/end
   - Interim arrangement while deeper work happens

**Stage 4: Separation Process (If Mediation Reveals Incompatibility)**

If mediation shows collaboration isn't sustainable:
- See Section 4: Exit Protocols

---

### 3.3 Unacceptable Behaviors

We commit to addressing immediately if either steward:
- Lies about financial matters
- Undermines other steward with clients or publicly
- Violates confidentiality agreements
- Engages in discriminatory behavior
- Creates unsafe conditions (emotionally, physically)

**Response**: Immediate conversation required. If behavior continues, escalate to mediation or separation.

---

### 3.4 Conflict With Clients

When conflict arises with clients:
1. Inform other steward immediately
2. Assess together: Is this resolvable or do we exit engagement?
3. Both stewards support resolution or exit decision
4. If client conflict affects one steward but not other, discuss whether:
   - Both exit (solidarity)
   - One exits, other continues (if appropriate)
   - We complete work despite conflict (holding boundaries)

## 4. EXIT PROTOCOLS

### 4.1 When To Consider Exiting Collaboration

Either steward can initiate exit conversation if:
- Fundamental values misalignment emerges
- Collaboration consistently creates suffering (despite resolution attempts)
- Financial model isn't working and cannot be adjusted
- Life circumstances require different priorities
- Work no longer feels nourishing or meaningful
- Unresolvable conflict persists

**Exit is not failure**. Partnerships have seasons. Ending well is success.

---

### 4.2 Exit Conversation Process

**Step 1: Name the Consideration (Week 1)**
- Steward considering exit shares this with other steward
- Describe what's leading to consideration
- Ask: Is there path to staying, or is exit right choice?

**Step 2: Exploration (Week 2-3)**  
- Both stewards reflect on whether partnership can continue
- Explore adjustments that might work (reduced capacity, role changes, etc.)
- Consult with trusted advisors/friends individually
- Decide: Try adjustment or proceed with exit

**Step 3: Decision (Week 4)**
- Meet to state decision clearly
- If staying with adjustments: Document what changes
- If exiting: Move to separation protocols

---

### 4.3 Separation Protocols

**Immediate Questions**:
- Timeline: When does collaboration end? (Ideally 3-6 month transition)
- Active clients: How do we complete current work? Who continues what?
- Financial: How do we settle accounts?
- Brand: Who uses "Limicelia" name going forward?

**Client Communication**:
- Together craft message to clients explaining transition
- Offer options: Continue with one steward, get referral, or complete current work and end
- Ensure no clients feel abandoned

**Financial Settlement**:
- Complete current client work, distribute revenue as agreed
- Settle any outstanding expenses
- Divide any reserves/savings according to contributions
- Close shared accounts

**Intellectual Property**:
- Materials created together: Both can use
- Individual content: Belongs to creator
- Limicelia brand/name: [To be decided at separation - could be:
  - Neither uses it (brand sunsets)
  - One continues with other's permission
  - Both use with clarification (Limicelia - Carol Xu; Limicelia - BK Dubé)]

**Knowledge Commons (Present of Work)**:
- All materials remain open-source
- Both can continue participating if commons exists
- Neither owns it (it's commons)

---

### 4.4 Graceful Closing

If collaboration ends:
- Public statement (on website/social) explaining transition
- Gratitude for what was built together
- Clear next steps for each steward's work
- No blame, no drama, just honest completion

**We commit to**: Ending well if we must end. Honoring what was built. Speaking truthfully and kindly about collaboration afterward.

---

## 5. SPECIAL PROVISIONS

### 5.1 Life Circumstances

**Major Life Changes Requiring Capacity Adjustment**:
- Pregnancy/childbirth/adoption: Adjust capacity and compensation as needed
- Serious illness (self or family): Immediate capacity reduction, other steward covers or pauses
- Divorce/major relationship change: Space for capacity reduction without judgment  
- Death of loved one: Full flexibility for grieving
- Geographic relocation: Assess whether virtual work continues or requires adjustment

**Process**: Affected steward informs other as soon as possible. We adjust without penalty or guilt.

---

### 5.2 Disagreements on Client Work

**If we disagree about accepting client**:
- Default: We say no (both must consent to use Limicelia brand)
- Exception: If one steward feels strongly, they can pursue under individual name

**If we disagree about approach with client**:
- Lead steward has final say on facilitation approach
- Support steward can decline to participate if fundamentally misaligned
- We discuss learning afterward

---

### 5.3 New Opportunities & Experiments

**Limicelia-Related Opportunities** (speaking, writing, partnerships):
- If using Limicelia brand: Discuss and get consent
- If compensation involved: Share revenue or explain why different split appropriate
- If individual opportunity: Each steward acts independently

**Present of Work Leadership**:
- Each steward participates according to own capacity and interests
- No requirement to be equally involved
- Support each other's experiments without requiring sameness
