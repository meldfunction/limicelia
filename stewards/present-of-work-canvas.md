## PRESENT OF WORK: LEAN STRATEGY CANVAS (PROVISIONAL)

**Goal**: Create practitioner knowledge commons exploring work as participation in life, strengthening practitioners' capacity to show up in transformation work while building shared resources for the field.

**Obstacle**: Practitioners burned out on extractive consulting lack visible alternatives. No infrastructure for practitioners to explore spiritual/livelihood integration, practice collective governance, or access peer learning outside transactional models.

**Strategy - Where We Play**: 
Transformation practitioners (facilitators, coaches, organizational developers) questioning extractive models and seeking:
- Spiritual/livelihood integration
- Peer learning communities
- Collective practice spaces
- Knowledge sharing without commercialization

**Strategy - How We Win**: 
Organize as actual commons - gift-stewarded spaces, peer learning, transparent decision-making. Practitioners don't learn *about* gift economy - they practice it. Content (blog, podcast, toolkit) is freely shared. Monthly gatherings use gift economy. This is slow-cooking model strengthening practitioners' field presence, not enriching organizers.
