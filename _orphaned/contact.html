Limicelia · Contact

This is not
a sales call.

The first conversation is genuine inquiry — including whether Limicelia is the right fit. We will tell you if we're not, and we'll point you toward someone who might be if we know them. Tell us what's happening.

01 / Reach out

Email us directly. Tell us what's happening and what you're trying to figure out. No intake form, no discovery call scheduling link. Just a genuine conversation.

hello@limicelia.org

We read everything and respond to every inquiry, usually within a few days. If we're in the middle of something intensive, it might take a little longer.

02 / What to say

The most useful thing to tell us is what's actually happening — not what you think you need. Some of the most important conversations start with someone saying "I'm not sure what we need, but something isn't working."

If it helps, you might tell us:

  • What kind of organization you're in
  • What's been happening and for how long
  • What you've already tried
  • What you're hoping the conversation might open
  • Any constraints on timing or budget worth naming early

03 / The name

Limicelia

limen

threshold · the space of crossing

mycelia

underground networks enabling exchange between organisms

Thresholds are the moments organizations can't navigate alone — succession and rupture, rapid growth, structural decay, the gap between stated values and lived practice. Mycelia are what make exchange possible underground, before anything visible happens above.

We work at both levels. The visible and the underground. The presenting problem and the structure underneath it.
