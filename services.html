We don't prescribe a practice. We enter what's actually happening and stay until something shifts. Every engagement begins with a diagnostic conversation — not a service selection.

01

Organizational Design & Governance

A board meeting where the same three people make every decision. Staff who know the answer but have no place to put it. An org chart that doesn't match how anything actually works.

↓ open

Sociocratic and participatory structures, decision-making frameworks, role clarity and accountability systems that reflect how the organization actually wants to operate — not how the org chart says it should. We build structure from the inside out, with the people who will live in it.

Leadership & Culture Transformation

The executive who says all the right things in the all-hands and then does the opposite in 1:1s. The team that's exhausted not from the work but from the gap between what's said and what's real.

↓ open

Working with leaders and teams through inner and outer work simultaneously — shifting culture, healing relational dynamics, building conditions for sustained change without burning out the people doing it. The practitioner's body and presence are instruments of this work.

Conflict Resolution & Coalition Building

Two departments that haven't spoken honestly in eighteen months. A coalition held together by shared values but fracturing on who holds the money. A rupture nobody will name in a meeting.

↓ open

Facilitated processes for navigating conflict, cross-sector tension, and coalition formation where power differentials are real and need to be named, not managed. We don't mediate toward comfort — we move toward clarity.

Organizational Memory & Knowledge

The founding director who held everything in her head for twelve years. The new ED on month three, realizing there are no documents for how any of this actually works.

↓ open

Capturing, stewarding, and activating institutional knowledge — especially in leadership transitions where what was learned must not leave with the person who's leaving. We make tacit knowledge legible without making it lifeless.

05

Community Engagement & Participatory Design

A city agency that held twelve listening sessions and changed nothing. A product team that surveyed thousands of users and built what they already planned. Community voice, laundered into legitimacy.

↓ open

Bringing community intelligence into the center of strategy, service, and product — not as consultation theater but as actual co-design where what the community knows shapes what gets built. This is distinct from user research. It is a different relationship to who holds authority over the design.

How to enter

We start with
what's breaking.

Diagnostic

What's actually wrong. Deep listening, honest diagnosis. For organizations who know something isn't working but aren't sure what it needs. Deliverable: a diagnostic report with recommended pathways — not a sales pitch for what comes next.

Transformation

Changing how you work. Discovery through harvest. Redesigned structures, practices, and capacity that the organization owns after we leave. We don't hand over a report. We build something with you and then step back.

Anchor Partner

Sustained thinking partner. For organizations navigating continuous transformation. Meta-organizations, complex coalitions, and institutions where a single project model won't hold. We stay long enough to see the whole arc.

Not sure
which fits
your situation?

Tell us what's breaking. We'll figure out together what it needs. The first conversation is genuine inquiry — not a pitch.
