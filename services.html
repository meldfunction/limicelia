Limicelia · Services

Wherever you start
is the right place.

Clients don't arrive with a service in mind. They arrive with a situation — something that isn't working, something that needs to be built, something that has shifted in a way they can't yet name. We begin there. The work finds its shape from what the situation calls for.

"We've restructured twice and the same problem keeps coming back."

"Our coalition keeps meeting but nothing actually moves."

"We have the values. The culture doesn't match them."

"The people doing this work are running out of ground to stand on."

Depending on what a situation calls for, the work moves through different modes — listening and diagnosing, accompanying and redesigning, convening across boundaries, developing the people inside the work. In practice these overlap. A single engagement might involve all of them. If it helps to have a picture of how these modes relate, the double diamond is one way to hold it.

Work we do

Conflict Navigation & Repair

Established With Colmenar Cooperative Consulting

Conflict carries information the organization hasn't found another way to say. Working with it as information — not just making it stop — is the difference between resolving a dispute and understanding what created it.

You might be saying —

"A founding team split we haven't recovered from" "Board and executive at an impasse" "A values conflict that went public" "We've tried mediation before and it didn't hold" "Trust broke during restructuring" "Power dynamics nobody will name"
  • Transformative mediation — focused on relationships over resolutions, holding the presenting dispute and its structural roots simultaneously
  • Restorative circles — everyone affected present, moving at the pace required for genuine acknowledgment before agreement
  • Post-rupture accompaniment — staying through the rebuilding of trust, not just the initial intervention
  • Conflict navigation system design — building the organization's own capacity to hold future conflict
Who leadsColmenar Cooperative Consulting leads most engagements. Limicelia co-delivers when conflict is part of a longer arc. We refer without condition.

Organizational Health & Governance

Active

Structure produces behavior. What the organization is experiencing is not random — it's the predictable output of how the system is built. Change the structure, and the behavior follows.

You might be saying —

"We scaled fast and lost ourselves" "The org chart doesn't match how power flows" "We're not practicing what we preach" "We keep losing good people" "Board and staff can't find each other"
  • Organizational health assessment — frontline interviews before leadership, structural analysis, diagnosis delivered to the whole organization
  • Governance redesign — participatory restructuring of decision-making, role clarity, and accountability systems drawing from sociocratic practice
  • Values-practice gap work — for organizations whose public commitments have outrun their internal practice
  • Leadership transition accompaniment — present through succession and the organizational grief it carries, not just the logistics
Who leadsBK and Carol Xu · Work includes: large government institutions, technology organizations, cooperative networks

Systems Convening

Actively growing

A well-designed convening shifts how people see each other and the system they share. Most don't. The difference is whether the gathering was designed to extract what the facilitator wanted to find, or to surface what the system already knows.

We've delivered elements of this work — Warm Data Labs, multi-stakeholder convenings, coalition design — and are building toward full sustained Systems Convening engagements. If this describes what you need, reach out now.

You might be saying —

"Our coalition meets but nothing moves" "We want community voice, not a survey" "The same twelve people keep showing up" "Organizations are working in parallel and missing each other" "We funded engagement and nothing changed"
  • Warm Data Labs — groups of 12 to 500+ exploring complexity by moving between conversational contexts, surfacing the relationships conventional data misses. Held at Harvard, the EU Parliament, and with Microsoft's AI team.
  • Multi-stakeholder convenings — designed for the specific relational and power dynamics in the room, not lifted from a template
  • Participatory community design — genuine co-creation with community voice at the center, not consultation that produces a report
  • Coalition and field-building — gatherings that shift how a network sees itself and what becomes possible between its members
Who leadsBK (Warm Data certified host) and Carol Xu · Cooperative-sector convenings with Colmenar

Leadership & Relational Development

Active

For those who believe the work should be changing the person doing it as much as the organization it's aimed at. When the skills are there but the ground isn't deep enough yet to hold them — and when the work itself is depleting faster than it's restoring.

You might be saying —

"The team hasn't recovered from six months ago" "We committed to equity and nothing changed" "We need a retreat that isn't theater" "Good people keep leaving" "Information is transmitted but not integrated" "People attend and nothing changes on Monday"
  • Team retreats and container design — real conversation about real dynamics, built from what the team is actually carrying rather than an agenda written in advance
  • Relational repair — accompaniment through rebuilding trust after rupture, not mediation of a specific dispute
  • Experiential course design — learning experiences where format and content are co-designed, built around what actually needs to change
  • Developmental coaching — for leaders in transition, relational and developmental rather than performance-focused

02 / How to engage

The work takes
different shapes.

Most engagements begin with a genuine conversation about what's happening — not a needs assessment, not a sales call. We figure out together what the situation calls for, and what shape of engagement that implies.

Diagnostic

What's actually happening

A few weeks

Deep listening, honest diagnosis. We talk to the people closest to the work before we talk to leadership. The output is a clear picture of what the system is producing and why — not a recommendation deck. The right starting point for most engagements.

Sustained engagement

Changing how you work

Months, not weeks

Full arc from discovery through harvest. Redesigned structures and practices the organization owns after we leave. Scope shaped by what the diagnostic reveals — not a pre-specified deliverable list.

Long-term presence

A thinking partner over time

A year or more

For organizations navigating continuous transformation — a Limicelia steward as sustained presence through multiple passages. Not a project. A relationship. We stay until you can cross without us.

The first conversation is not a sales call. It's genuine inquiry — including whether Limicelia is the right fit. We will tell you if we're not. We price on a sliding scale based on what an organization can sustain, not market rate. Fees are discussed after we understand what the situation calls for. Say early if budget is a real constraint.
