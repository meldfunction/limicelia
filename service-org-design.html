Limicelia · Organizational Design & Governance

When the structure

For organizations that have grown faster than their governance, or changed values without changing how decisions get made. We build structures that reflect how the organization actually wants to operate — not how the org chart says it should.

01 / When you need this

Situations that
bring people here.

You know you need this work when a structural problem keeps appearing in relational form.

"We keep making the same decisions in the same way and getting the same outcomes. Nobody can name who is actually accountable for what."

Accountability gap

"We went from five people to twenty-five in two years. The way we used to decide things doesn't work anymore but we haven't replaced it with anything."

Growth without governance

"We say we are participatory but decisions are actually made by three people before any meeting happens. Staff know this and are disengaged."

Values-structure gap

"We are a coalition of organizations with different cultures trying to make joint decisions. There is no shared structure for how we do that and it is grinding us down."

02 / What we actually do

Four types of
engagement.

Governance
Structure Design

Working with leadership and staff to map how decisions are currently made, where accountability is unclear, and what structures — roles, circles, consent processes — would actually fit how this organization wants to operate. We do not arrive with a framework and install it. We build it from the inside out, with the people who will live in it.

Typical: 3–4 months · Includes facilitated sessions + documentation + implementation support

Role Clarity
& Accountability

Many organizations have roles that exist on paper but don't match how work actually flows. We surface the gap between the formal structure and the informal one, then redesign roles that reflect real accountability — including where authority lives, where it doesn't, and who needs to be in which conversations.

Typical: 6–10 weeks · Usually pairs with governance structure work

Decision-Making
Process Design

Not all decisions need the same process. We help organizations build a differentiated decision-making system — consent-based for some, consultative for others, executive for others — with clear criteria for which process applies when. The goal is less energy spent deciding how to decide, more capacity for the actual work.

Typical: 4–8 weeks · Often a component of governance structure work

Governance
Transition Support

When an organization is moving through a structural change — new leadership, merger, shift from founder-led to distributed governance — we support the transition itself, not just the destination. This means accompanying the organization through the discomfort of the in-between, not just designing the structure it is moving toward.

Typical: 6–9 months · Intensive accompaniment phase

03 / What's different here

Structure without
the practitioner inside it
doesn't hold.

Most governance work fails at implementation — not because the design was wrong but because nobody stayed for the part where the culture has to change alongside the structure.

Most org design consulting

  • Arrives with a preferred framework (Holacracy, RACI, etc.) and adapts the org to fit it
  • Produces a governance document and exits
  • Works primarily with senior leadership
  • Treats implementation as the client's problem
  • Measures success at handoff, not six months later

Limicelia

  • Builds structure from what the organization actually is, not what a framework says it should be
  • Stays through implementation — the document is a byproduct, not the deliverable
  • Brings the people closest to the work into the design
  • Treats resistance as information, not as an obstacle
  • Measures success when the structure is self-sustaining
